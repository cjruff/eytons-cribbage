Who's playing?

The computer plays your seat. Games count toward this player's real stats and achievements.

A game is filed under its strongest computer's level, and also under each family opponent who played.

Backups (moving to another device)

Loading a backup replaces players with the same name, and adds any new ones.

Bring in your stats from the Windows (Python) version

On the Windows PC, the files are in C:\Users\<your name>\.pycribbage: stats.json, achievements.json and settings.json. Pick any of them (hold Ctrl to pick several). They replace this player's stats and achievements. Tip: in the file picker, type %USERPROFILE%\.pycribbage in the address bar to jump there.
